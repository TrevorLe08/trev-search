## Rules that were skipped, impossible or missing (now fixed)

1. **Date check could block everything.** The date search returned cached pages (5/8/2026) against the session date 16/10/2026 (Friday, correct). Read literally, the rule meant "stop". Now: the session date wins; search only when the session has no date.
2. **Search-tool summaries looked like sources.** They mixed real pages with rumors (a "Reuters" story that GPT-6.1 was shelved). Now: level 3 until the page is opened.
3. **Wikipedia had no rule.** Now: orientation, follow its citations, uncited = level 2.
4. **Blocked sites.** OpenAI, Britannica, Feynman Lectures, IOP, toureiffel.paris, Reuters returned 403 to the script and often to WebFetch. Now: script → fetch tool → browser, and stop at bot-check pages.
5. **Content farms pass the link check.** `q15tp4m.example-farm.com/article/openai-releases...` and `blog.example-farm.com/history/...` returned `ok`. Now: named patterns in the vetting rules.
6. **Header dates.** danchuphapluat showed "14/07/2026" in search but was published 2022-12-02; tag pages matched the date anchor. Now: read the article's own date; listing pages are not sources.
7. **"Reachable" was treated like "read".** Now: only read content supports claims and counts toward source targets.
8. **"Điều 7" false match.** `--find` hit Article 7 of the amending decree, not of NĐ 168. Now: check the sentence around a match.
9. **DNS failure ≠ nonexistent.** A real archive was briefly unresolvable. The script now says `unreachable` (DNS, timeout, 5xx) separately from `dead` (404/410), and check-fast says "không truy cập được lúc kiểm tra".
10. **Redirected/lookalike URLs** (`en.wikipedia.com`): list the final URL.
11. **search-url balance** on contested topics: sources from the main sides, labelled.
12. **Anchoring stable topics** pulled in SEO pages: anchors only on current-state parts.
13. **Pets** had no authorities in topic routing; added, with the domain-filter tip.
