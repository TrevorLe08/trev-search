# Topic routing

Where each kind of fact lives. Examples, not a closed list: verify the publisher's identity (lookalike domains exist) and prefer the body that owns the fact. Primary = owns or originally records the fact; secondary = reliable for context and cross-checking. A row can hold claims of every freshness class (SKILL.md, Time and dates): the class belongs to the claim, not to the row. Vietnamese official names are kept in parentheses because they are the search terms.

| Domain | Primary sources | Secondary sources | Watch for |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Technology, software, AI | Official docs, release notes/changelogs, vendor blogs, GitHub repos/issues, standards (RFC, W3C, ISO) | Engineering blogs, reputable tech press, benchmarks with published method | Version mismatch; vendor benchmarks are vendor claims; fast change, always anchor |
| Products and prices | Manufacturer/official store pages, official spec sheets | Major retailers, independent reviews and tests | Price varies by region, store and date; promotional vs list price; note the snapshot date |
| Natural sciences, math, academic subjects | Textbooks and university course pages, encyclopedias of the field, peer-reviewed papers, standards bodies (e.g. IUPAC, BIPM) | Reputable science journalism, educational sites (Khan Academy, Britannica) | Popular articles oversimplify; a single study is not consensus; preprints are not peer-reviewed |
| Health, medicine, nutrition | WHO, national health ministries (Vietnam: Ministry of Health, Bộ Y tế), CDC/NIH/NHS, clinical guidelines, peer-reviewed reviews | Hospital and medical-school patient pages, reputable health press | No individualized advice; supplements/marketing claims; jurisdiction-specific drug approvals; guidelines are revised, so use the current version and never show outdated advice as current |
| History | Primary documents, archives, museums, scholarly books and journals, critical editions | Encyclopedias, university history pages, reputable documentaries | Contested interpretations: attribute each; dates in different calendars; nationalist or propaganda sources |
| Culture, arts, religion, language | Official cultural institutions, museums, religious bodies' own texts, dictionaries and language academies, scholarly works | Reputable cultural press, encyclopedias | Folk claims and viral myths; one community's practice is not universal; describe beliefs without endorsing |
| Politics, current affairs | Official government/party/parliament sites, official gazettes, election commissions, transcripts | Several reputable outlets across viewpoints, wire services (Reuters, AP, AFP), fact-checkers | Stay neutral; separate statement from fact; breaking news changes fast; state-run vs independent outlets |
| Economics, finance, markets | Statistics offices, central banks, ministries of finance, IMF/World Bank/OECD, company filings and investor-relations pages, stock exchanges | Financial press, research-house reports | Measurement period vs publication date; nominal vs real; calendar vs fiscal quarter; no investment advice |
| Law, regulations, administrative procedures | Official legal-text portals and gazettes (Vietnam: Official Gazette, Công báo; National Legal Documents Portal, Cổng thông tin văn bản pháp luật quốc gia; National Public Service Portal, Cổng dịch vụ công), ministries' official pages | Legal databases and law-firm explainers | Effective date vs signing date; check status (in force, amended, replaced, repealed) on the official portal, not the text's age; jurisdiction; not legal advice |
| Work, employment, careers, small business | Labour and social-insurance authorities, tax authorities, official job and tender portals, professional associations and licensing bodies, ILO | Reputable business and careers press, established HR and accounting explainers | Labour law, salary, insurance and tax rules depend on jurisdiction and effective date; job ads and recruiter posts are leads; job and investment scams |
| Agriculture, fisheries, food production | Agriculture ministries and extension services, FAO, agricultural universities and research institutes, official market-price bulletins, plant-protection and veterinary authorities | Reputable farming press, cooperatives and producer associations | Prices are local and daily; pesticide, fertilizer and drug approvals change (check status); advice from sellers of the product is marketing |
| Engineering, construction, technical trades | Standards bodies (ISO, IEC, national standards), building and electrical codes, manufacturer datasheets and installation manuals, professional engineering bodies | Trade press, university course pages, experienced-practitioner guides | Which edition of a standard or code applies and whether it was replaced; vendor datasheets are vendor claims; safety rules come from the code, not forums |
| Transport, driving, vehicles | Transport ministries and traffic police, official road-law texts, vehicle manufacturers' manuals and recall notices | Reputable motoring press, driving-school material | Fines and rules change with new decrees; jurisdiction; recalls are per model and year |
| Education, admissions, scholarships | Official university/ministry pages, scholarship provider's own page | Reputable education press, student forums (leads only) | Deadlines and quotas change every year; last year's notice is not this year's |
| Pets and animals | Veterinary schools and hospitals (e.g. Cornell Feline Health Center), veterinary associations, animal-welfare charities (PDSA, International Cat Care), national veterinary authorities | Vet-written guides from clinics and pet insurers | Results in Vietnamese are dominated by shops and SEO sites; filter to these domains, then use Vietnamese sources only from vets, clinics or veterinary faculties |
| Everyday life (food, travel, home, beauty, hobbies) | Official tourism/transport/venue pages, food-safety and consumer authorities, manufacturer instructions | Reputable lifestyle press, established guides, review platforms, community experience | Opening hours, prices and rules change; reviews are opinions; safety advice from authorities wins |
| Sports, entertainment, games | Official league/federation/club/studio/platform sites, official social accounts | Reputable sports and entertainment press, stats databases | Rumors and transfer gossip; live results need timestamps |
| Geography, environment, weather, disasters | National meteorological and disaster agencies, UN/UNEP/IPCC, official maps and statistics | Reputable science and news outlets | Forecasts are not facts; region-specific data; live data needs timestamps |
| Public figures, organizations | Their official sites and verified accounts, official registries, reputable profiles | Encyclopedias, reputable press | Current role may have changed; impostor accounts; do not research private individuals |
| Encyclopedias (any domain) | — | Wikipedia, Britannica, field encyclopedias: orientation and a map to original sources | Follow their citations; uncited sentences are level 2 at best; many big sites block scripts, so open them in a browser |
| Vietnam-specific topics | `.gov.vn` sites of the responsible body, provincial portals, official press of the body | Major Vietnamese outlets (VnExpress, Tuổi Trẻ, Thanh Niên, VTV, VietnamPlus...) | Administrative changes (merged provinces, renamed agencies); search in Vietnamese first |

## Unlisted topic

1. Ask who would own or originally record this fact (an authority, an institution, the creator, a dataset, a primary document). Search for that body by name.
2. Find one recognized reference work or expert body for context.
3. Cross-check with one independent reputable outlet.
4. Use community sources only for experience or leads, labelled as such.
5. Still nothing reliable: say so and give the closest reliable source with its limits.
